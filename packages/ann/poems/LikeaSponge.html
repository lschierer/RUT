Like a Sponge

Dear sweet Jesus, please make my soul like a sponge.
Take it,sorrowful and sinful, and plunge
It into Your Spirit's deep loving ocean
So that my only conscious notion
Is that of my soul being
In the presence of You Who is all seeing.
Let Your love surround me.
Let Your love saturate me.
I long to lose myself
Into Your Mystical Body's stealth.

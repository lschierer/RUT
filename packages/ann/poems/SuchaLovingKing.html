Such a Loving King

 

Such a Loving King

Jesus was slapped and adorned
With a crown of sharp thorns
Such a loving King
To endure such suffering

Jesus was spat upon, scourged
And mocked with harsh words
Such a loving King
To endure such suffering

Jesus was crucified
Stabbed in the side and died
Such a merciful, loving King
To be our sacrificial offering!

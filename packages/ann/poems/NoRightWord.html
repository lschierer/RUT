No Right Word
Dear Jesus, my sweet Savior and dearest friend
Your Love for me is without exception and has no end

My sins caused You Brain crushing, Blood rushing agony in a garden
Please have mercy on me and grant me pardon

You bravely bore a crown of sorrows and Willingly carried my heavy cross
Please know how sorry i am for your burning coldness and Blood loss

My sins still betray You and beat You, and bitterly bash You repeatedly
Please forgive me for i am so extremely guilty

For all the wounds my sins will still cause, there is no right word
Please still love me in Your Way that to most may seem absolutely absurd

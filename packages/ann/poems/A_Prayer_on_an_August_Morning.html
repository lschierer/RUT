A Prayer on an August Morning



The salty air is so very still and already so very warm
In the delightful, melodic dawning of this mid August Morn.
Coral pink smears of clouds whisper across the baby blue sky
To tell all the Keys that the Son has risen and is moving up high.
The sea is visibly so especially calm and so very extremely clear
Reflecting the Son’s Light like a bride’s and her mom’s shared, sparkling tears.
I silently pray as I slowly breathe in and out while I stand on the grayed deck
That all my thoughts and deeds in some way, even if it is small, may reflect
The freely given Love of our Father Who gives us so much and can give us no more
Better gifts than those of the most Holy Eucharist and Heaven’s opened door!

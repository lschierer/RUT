Sure and Lasting

I quietly hold in my heart this love that is sure and lasting
Full of the Son’s Light in my world so darkly contrasting
I sense my beloved’s protecting arms and strengthening words
Cradling me in the comfort of the One, Unseen and Unheard.

I am lured to a sweet lullaby land luminous in trust and hope
As a soft ocean breeze brushes the brow of a warm, sandy slope
My beloved’s presence is golden- etched in the glow of the moon
And I feel secure surrounded by angels singing a faith- filled tune.

I know our souls’ kinship will remain now and forever, near or far
And we will dance together in the countless lives of celestial stars
I pray that someday and soon we may again meet in a sweet retreat
With pink rose petals scattered about our side by side, sandy feet

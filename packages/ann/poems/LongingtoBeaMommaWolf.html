Longing to Be a Momma Wolf

I long to be a silent, solitary
Momma wolf who lovingly and wishfully
Watch her happy, playful puppies
While they frolic with giddiness and glee
As they romp and run in the starry
Son-lit snow with their beloved Daddy
In a shelter full of the Spirit's security
Of a stand of strong, White Birch trees!

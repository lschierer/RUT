Hanging Between the Lines

 

Hanging Between the Lines

Sometimes the line is dotted
Somtimes the line's been blotted
Today the line seems so very thin
Between life in God and deadly sin!

One thing i know with certainty
I know i need the Blessed Trinity
To be steadfast and strong
To hold me while i hang on

I pray for unwavering, unquestionable belief
Enough to get through this excruciating grief
I pray when God speaks to me in His quiet voice
That i will listen and choose life as my choice!

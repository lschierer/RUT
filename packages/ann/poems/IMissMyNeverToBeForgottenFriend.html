I Miss My Never To Be Forgotten Friend

My dear, never to be forgotten, fond friend
I miss you at the beginning, middle and end
Of each and every minute of each and every
Hour of each and every day. I miss your very
Presence and the pure essence of your kind being.
I miss looking forward to special times of seeing
You! I miss your kind and caring eyes filled
With God's sparkling grace. I miss feeling thrilled
To sit silently still with the Spirit in your presence!
I miss easily sharing our two souls' simple essence!
I miss giving you my writings filled with love
On loan to me from our generous God above!
Without you, i am like the ocean without its salt!
I am like an earthquake ready to break on a fault
As my heart has not healed from where it broke!
I am like a bicycle with too many missing spokes!
I pray that someday we may see each other again
As you will always be one of my most beloved men!

A Postcard- Perfect Dream

Beautiful, blissful sleep softly calls to me and gently beckons me
With a promise that a paradise festooned with palm trees
Is only a picture postcard- perfect dream away!
I can almost feel the balmy breeze that makes the coconut palms sway
And I can almost see the Hibiscus bushes bursting with blooms in brilliant
hues
As green anoles agilely scale their new stems and hide from view.
I can almost smell the sweetness of Gardenias and Jasmine joyfully floating
And I can almost feel the warm, wet sand squish between my toes groping
To find some buried treasured trinket
Like a baby sand dollar or a big sea biscuit!
This imagined tropical scene is complete with the enchanting, energizing
ocean
Calm and colored like a watercolor palette of greens and blues with the
golden
Son sharing His sparkling hope diamond studded, earth reaching, reassuring
rays
That He will be with me now and throughout all my lonely nights and days!

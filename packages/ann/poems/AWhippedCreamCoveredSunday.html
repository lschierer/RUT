A Whipped Cream Covered Sunday

God generously scooped up this delightful day
With sherbet hues rising high in the sky and sorbet
Coolness in the crystal glistening, early morning air
Thank You, God for all the tender loving care
That You so freely sprinkle like festive and gay
Colored jimmies on a whipped cream covered Sunday!

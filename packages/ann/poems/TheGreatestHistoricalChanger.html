The Greatest Historical Changer

Burning candles flickered on the fir tree silently
Brilliant, silver Christmas bells jingled joyously
A curious, cuddly cat curled up in a chair quietly
A huge yule log sputtered sparks spontaneously

A choo choo train chugged along the railroad track
Santa stopped at the stable and knelt with his sack
Adoring angels made sure the Babe did not lack
A star radiated in Love against the sky of black!

Astrologers gathered around and adored in a manger
A simple, Baby Boy Who sincerely knew no strangers
Who was not afraid of any aforementioned danger
Who would be the single, greatest historical Changer!

Sadly School is Starting

While most moms are excitedly gleeful and glad
My heart is heavy and i am so very, very sad.
Our splendid summer vacation is about to come to an end.
Starting school is unfortunately just around the bend.
I have tremendously treasured our summer times together.
We have thoroughly enjoyed this year's very hot weather!
I' ve liked our loose, leisurely and lounging lifestyle.
I' ll miss having my children hour after hour each day wile
Away. I' ve loved being surrounded by their physical presence.
I will mournfully miss their voices' resounding resonance.
My dear children will start this new school year older.
Soon the warm weather outside will start to get colder.
I pray my children will grow in God's wisdom and grace
To become giving and loving people in the human race.

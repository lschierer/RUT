A Cross of Golden Light

God is indeed truly awesome and amazing
For i can't believe that i'm gazing
At a cross made out of golden light
Shining bright in a starless night.
It softly sheds reassuring radiance
That with more time and more patience
God will give my heart still grieving
Enough peace, love, and hope for healing!

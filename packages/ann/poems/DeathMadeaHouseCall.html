Death Made a House Call

Death made a house call!
Hope made a withdrawal.
After blowing His flame out
The Spirit skipped out and about.
Mary's Son fled fast to follow Him.
Now the Light is so far and so dim.
The Father removed His hand.
I am no longer part of His plan.
Just an animal, that is all.
So why am i in this fearful freefall?

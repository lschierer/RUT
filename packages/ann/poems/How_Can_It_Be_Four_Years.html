How Can It Be Four Years




How can it be four years since you so suddenly died
You were my rock, the one in whom i solely relied
On for my strength, completeness, and clarity
In this fallen world filled with so much emptiness and insanity

There just were not enough words whispered with our eyes closed by the dark
There was not enough bicycle pedaling or playful picnics in the park
We did not get to share enough spontaneous splashes of sparkles and laughter
We did not get to share enough to have and to hold happiness ever after

I am so homesick for you to tenderly hold my hand real tight
And for you to gently kiss away my tears cascading with fright
I so long to simply be with you side by side in a timeless dimension
I pray for this with confidence in the merciful power of Jesus' Resurrection

While the Fall Harvest Sings Its Final Hymns




Leaves dangle from tree limbs in loquacious delight
Acorns smap and pop as they hit the earth with all their might
Chrysathemuns bloom in royal jewel colors befitting a king
And deer leap in Love\'s measure with liveliness and zing
While the Fall harvest bountifully sings its final hymns

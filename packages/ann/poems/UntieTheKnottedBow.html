Untie The Knotted Bow

When i get down and feel so very low
I must try to remember that life is a gift
>From God. I must untie the knotted bow
Tear off the taped paper and gently lift
Off my small, simple box’s ordinary lid
To look and truly see everything inside
To discover all that our good God did
Because His Love is so mercifully wide!

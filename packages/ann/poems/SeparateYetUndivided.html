Separate Yet Undivided

Severe lightning storms attacked the sea and thunderously clashed.
Tormented, white- capped waves thrashed out and crashed.
Two, separate souls were tossed up onto the same silvery shore.
Their two, separate hearts touched in a way they could not ignore.
Their close, comforting encounter connected and forever changed
Their lives as true, lasting love was extended and exchanged.
Two separate lives are still shaped now by their bonded unity
With each other and undivided in the unending, Holy Trinity!

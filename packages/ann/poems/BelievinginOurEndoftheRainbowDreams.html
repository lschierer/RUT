Believing in Our End of the Rainbow Dreams

Will you hold my heart so very tenderly tonight
In the pillow soft shadows of the shiniest Starlight

Will you share your soul with me as we dance so dearly
With our sparkling eyes shut while we still see so clearly

Will you walk with me in waking wonder and hand in hand
Reflecting the honesty of the Risen Son in the Promised Land

I long to imagine us spending time closely and entirely together
Trusting in the silent song of the Spirit who flies without feathers
And to hope and believe in our end of the rainbow dreams
For in them, lies True, Living Love’s glowing, golden gleams

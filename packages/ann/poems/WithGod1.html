With God

God is my everything
My reason for existence.
His Love will help my soul sing.
His Dove will give me resistence
Against the evil one's presence.
With His loving Son's light
God will enlighten my essence
And i will feel His powerful might.
With Light as my Deliverer's Defence
I will be steadfast and strong
Against the adversary's offence.
My heart will shine with him gone!!

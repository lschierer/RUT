Starlight Falls

Shimmery starlight falls down upon me
Like sparkling drops of soft, misting rain.
It illuminates my dimmed mind in mourning
With love-filled dreams to drown my pain!

My loved ones occupy a special space
Within my sweetest and dearest dreams.
Their significant presence is a silent respite
>From hearing the painful, restless, screams!

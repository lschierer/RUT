In This Enchanted Darkness






As my longing soul goes strolling
Among the wistful stars in a wonderful, cosmic mystery
My hope sparkles in anticipation
That your soul will shoot across in a song so whispery
And in this enchanted darkness
Our two souls will, somehow, fantastically collide
Lighting a shimmery, happy trail
With our two hungering hearts opened wide.
Our sense of the ticking of time would be hushed
By the warm embrace of the crescent moon’s gleam
As we gaily midnight waltz to golden music
In a thought impossible, rainbow-colored, come true dream!

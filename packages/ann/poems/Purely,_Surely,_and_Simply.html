Purely, Surely, and Simply






A beginning and an end
Are found in the romantic sea
A song, a secret, a hanging star
A huge smile and small me
But not in our God, Who is
Who was, and Who will always be
His Essence is Love's existence
Ever pure, sure, and with simplicity!

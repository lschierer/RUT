Longing To Be a Hermit Crab

I long to be a happy, huge clawed, hermit crab
Living on a sliver of crushed coral, sandy beach
Spending my day in the shadows of the radiant Son
Resting and staying out of ravenous, brown pelicans’ reach
I would praise God as i make gay revelry and roam
Each night as the starshine scatters its bright glitter
I would thank Him so much for my simple, shell home
Near the salty sea as tiny tree frogs so sweetly twitter!

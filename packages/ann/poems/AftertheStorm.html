After the Storm

After the thunderous storm has swiftly swept by
After there are no more tumbling tears left to cry
After my requests have long been sent with no reply
I longingly look up towards the brilliant, blue sky
And implore the aid of the Mother of the Most High
My soul seeks to be set free of sin in order to fly

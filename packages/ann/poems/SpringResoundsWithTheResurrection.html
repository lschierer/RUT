Spring Resounds With The Resurrection

Spring resounds with the Resurrection of The Word
His gift of new life can be seen, felt, smelled, and heard
Cardinals courting, ducks diving, and sap seeping
Deer dancing, streams splashing, and Willows weeping
Bees buzzing, rivers running, and flowers unfolding
Sparrows singing, grass greening, and hearts holding
Sparkles of the Risen Son’s brilliant, white Light to share
With those whose wintered souls starve for someone to care!

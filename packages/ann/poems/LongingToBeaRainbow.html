Longing To Be a Rainbow

I long to be a softly shimmering hint of a rainbow
Gaily revealing the sugar and spice colors of Light
In the glint of the Risen Son’s eternal living glow
Giving glistening thanks for tremendous thunderstorms
That sweep the world clean and cause tributaries to flow
Giving prismatic praise to our gloriously good God
For the fresh fragrance of His Breath wherever i go

Of Whitest Wonderment







Fortuitous, fluffy flakes of fairly- like frozenness freefall
Gracefully to gently give glad grandeur to the grieving ground
While wee, whimsical wishes of whitest wonderment well up
As merry mittens make mute men out of marshmallow mounds

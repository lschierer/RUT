A Moonlit Delivery

In the middle of the loneliest night
I receive a miracle, a moonlit delivery
You arrive shining in the Son's delight
Full of the Spirit ‘s light sparkling silvery
Love cascades down a radiant rainbow
River whose living colors slowly spill
Into my solitary soul longing to glow
With God's gift of a new life and its thrill

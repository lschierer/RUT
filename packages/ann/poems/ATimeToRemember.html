A Time To Remember

I excitedly wait to see you, my friend
And then
Before i know it, it's goodbye again!
I hope and i pray for you to come
To bring God's energy, life, and wisdom.
When you arrive
My soul comes alive
The first time i hugged you
My soul fancifully and carefreely flew
Nonstop up to the bright, sunlit, blue
Sky and chased pillow-like clouds to
And fro while my heart exploded
In a pyrotechnic display loaded
With so much colorful, sparkling love
That could only be from our precious Lord above!
You bring the Spirit's joy and delight!
You bear God's Image for my sight!
You give His merciful compassion.
It pours out of you in unlimited rations.
I am very exceptionally blessed
That you agreed to be a guest
At my home on the 21st day of December
A day i will always fondly remember.

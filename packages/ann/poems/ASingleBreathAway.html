A Single Breath Away

In the silence, when there is not a single sound.
I know you're not gone, your spirit is around.
We're but a single breath away and a heartbeat apart.
I can feel your charity surround my sorrowing heart.

I can see your dark eyes in my mind so clear.
God's sparkle shines so to settle all my fear.
I truly trust your soul, sent by Divine chance.
I firmly believe with your hand, i will dance.

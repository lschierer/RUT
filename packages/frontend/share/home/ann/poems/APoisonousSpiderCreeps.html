A Poisonous Spider Creeps

A poisonous, pensive spider creeps about
Casting sticky strands of self doubt
To slyly spin into tightly woven webs to trap
My mournful mind so he can knavingly wrap
His locking legs around and devishly daunt
My every thought with terrifyingly, tempting taunts!
He attacks me when i am awake and when i sleep
so restlessly. He wreaks ravaging results. I weep!

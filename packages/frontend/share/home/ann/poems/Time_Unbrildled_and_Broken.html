Time Unbridled and Broken








I crave to hear your voice
To feel your breath in soft words spoken
I long for one moment together
Lasting forever, time unbridled and broken

I crave to see into your eyes
To feel the closeness of your distant face
I long to share our souls, simply
Warmth saturating this cold, empty space

God Keeps On Giving

Bethlehem
Our infinite God became a poor, helpless baby boy on a bed of straw
Nazareth
Our almighty King and God was an obedient Son and followed The Law
Jerusalem
Our God, Master of the Universe, became a lowly servant to wash men’s feet
Gethsemane
Our God, Love’s Perfection, was a man laden with our sins and death to greet
Calvary
Our merciful God became as a criminal hanging, dying, giving beyond any
cost!
Tabernacles all over the world
Our God of Love is the Bread Who waits so no one who comes will be lost!

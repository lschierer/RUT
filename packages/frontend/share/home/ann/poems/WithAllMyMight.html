With All My Might

 

With All My Might

I long to lay beside the blue ocean’s luminous Living Water
As it caresses the crushed coral sand while its waves saunter
Up onto a tired and thirsty tidal beach’s deserted expanse
And dangle my feet freely in its warm, flowing exuberance!

I long to feel our starving souls reach out softly to one another
Tenderly touching, hearts tingling as we firmly hold each other
While smelling the sweet fragrance of blushing yellow roses
Standing together against all odds, side by side, toes next to toes!

I wish with every single breath that i take and with all of my might
That you could simply stay forever in my near sight
That we could hold each others’ hands so very tight
Our souls soaring together like two tangled kites straight up to The Light!

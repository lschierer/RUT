Beyond Belief

 

Beyond Belief

I pray for The Light to shine through this darkest night
Screaming voices surround me with their fears and fright
My skin crawls with intense craving and burns with desire
To be touched, to be tenderly caressed, to feel the fire
In your heart burn brilliantly, radiantly, and lovingly
Sometimes firmly, sometimes gently, but always lovingly
I am so lost, so filled with languish, so extremely lonely
My sorrowful soul searches for some sign, something simply
That i can sense while i am locked up in this limited body
I am longing to know beyond belief that you loved me truly

i will still dance
AMDG
i will still dance!!!

 

i will still dance!!!

I've lost control of my senses.
It's as if i am surrounded by fences.
I can't see over the vast,endless horizon.
My retinas have been blinded by the risen Son!
I can no longer hear your soft whisperings.
The music that i hear only brings
Many memories and wistful wishes for what can't be
For i long and dream of you touching me.

I long To feel your hand holding mine.
I dream of feeling our toes entwined.
I dream of feeling your arms reaching across my chest.
I long to just lay down beside you and rest!

Because of your love, I will still dance
And i will give my life another chance.
I will look for your love in our children's faces
And in our usual and familiar spaces.
I will also try to find God's infinite love
For my complete healing must come from Him above!

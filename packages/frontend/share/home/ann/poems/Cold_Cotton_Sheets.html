Cold Cotton Sheets

Cold Cotton Sheets




When i turn off the radio playing Christian Rock music
When i turn down my stinging cold, cotton jersey sheets
When my bedroom's shadows fade into the darkness
Pounding like a jack hammer, my heavy heart beats

My desirous desperation to see you and be with you
Becomes a big foot- smashing, face- bashing giant
Trying to stomp out my soul's flickering torch of hope
Thrashing about my crushed carcess in my mind not silent

Carrying with him the lightning power of Eternal Love
My vigilant, white feathered guard veers my way
As does the Holy Comforter come swiftly winging by
To calm my imagination gone haywire and astray

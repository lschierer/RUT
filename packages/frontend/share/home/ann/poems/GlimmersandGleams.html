Glimmers and Gleams

God is beginning to dwell in my desolate dreams
I am beginning to see glimmers and gleams
Of His goodness, splendor, and lasting peace.
Noxious nightmares are beginning to finally cease.

God's consistent, compassionate kindness causes
My heart to swell with so much love despite my losses.
The Spirit conceals His peace in the quiet
Solitude that replaces my sorrowing soul's riot.

His Light shines with an invitation to end my rife
To begin each day in The Way, The Truth and The Life
My great God can give me nothing better than this
To dance with Him in the sacrament of the Holy Eucharist.

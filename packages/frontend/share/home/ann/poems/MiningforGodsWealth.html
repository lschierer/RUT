Mining for God's Wealth

 

Mining for God's Wealth

You were the expert miner
I was the brand new mine
You went in deep exploring
And found a treasure of ore
It took much effort, time
And hard work on your part
You brought to the surface
God's vast, hidden wealth
Now the mine is abandoned
And the shafts are unused
But the mine is not empty
There's plenty to unearth
The entrance is not bolted
New miners are much in need!

For Your Twenty-Fourth Anniversary of Being a Missionary Servant of the Most Holy Trinity




Twenty-four years of being a merciful missionary priest
Twenty- four years of serving and sacrificing at the Eucharistic Feast
Twenty- four years of unselfish service in the one, Holy, Catholic Church
Twenty- four years of tirelessly helping others on their journey and search

Twenty- four years of personally growing in Love by personally giving Love
Twenty- four years of humbling yourself, having hope in the Trinity above
Twenty- four years of preaching and practicing the Good News
Twenty- four years of loving all people of all creeds and hues

Blessings and happiness on this May 16, 2005, your twenty- fourth anniversary
To a good priest, my forever, fond friend, and my personal missionary
May the most adorable Trinity never cease in Love to increasingly amaze you
May Love give you His Grace to strengthen and sustain you in everything you do

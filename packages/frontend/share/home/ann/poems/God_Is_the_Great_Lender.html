God is the Great Lender

 

God is the Great Lender

Ownership of everything belongs to God, not me.
He is the Great Lender with great generosity.
I thank God for the precious time He lent.
I thank God for THAT cherished time we spent
Using His Perfect Love to love one another
And watching that Love spread to so many others
Not being diminished or lessoned in any way
But growing with a flourish with each lent day!

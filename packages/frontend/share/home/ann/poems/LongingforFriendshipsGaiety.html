Longing for Friendship's Gaiety

I long to gaze into your dark eyes that so sparkle
Like the lights on a game of chance at a carnival.
I long to be with you to spend some together time
To get off this racing, convoluted roller coaster, back into line.
I long to hear your voice, sometimes quiet as the wind.
You always calm my fears that fly off a ferris wheel in a tailspin.
I long to hold and hug you with purity in our hearts.
Your embrace helps to erase the sharp pain from darts
That grief and loneliness send flying through the air singly
To pop my hopeful balloon before it's been filled completely.
I long to share God's love, fully, free, and simply
To share our gift of friendship with spontaneity and gaiety!

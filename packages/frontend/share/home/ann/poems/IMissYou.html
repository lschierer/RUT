AMDG
I Miss You!!

 

I Miss You!!

I want you to come back.
I can't change that fact.
I know you are really dead
But you're still in my head
And heart and mind and soul.
Without you,i'm less than whole!
There's no end to my grief.
There's no sign of relief.
It's difficult to sleep
My sorrow is too deep!!
I have terrible dreams!!
Do you hear my screams??

You Can Never Be Far From My Heart

You Can Never Be Far From My Heart






No superficial or supernatural distance could ever keep us apart
You are never far from the space where i hold you in my heart
No sustaining, hurricane force winds or raging, storm surging ocean
Could possibly pull more strongly than my love moving in God's Motion

Though we seem to search separately and and in different directions
In the Spirit, our souls still can and do make such sparkling connections
Your presence keeps pushing the pieces of my broken heart together
And i hear you whisper softly of our sweet union in Heaven forever

In my loneliness, i know you are near
In sorrowful silence, i shed a happy tear

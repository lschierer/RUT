A Letter to My Son




My dearest, beloved son
Why does our time together run
Where does it go
I wish it would go so much more slow!

Your short life so far has been chock- full of change
Dying and death have not been strange
Yet Divine Providence has provided enough for you
And you may be confident that He will continue to

You are a bright, rising, young gentleman now
I pray you grow in God’s wisely know-how
I pray you are strengthened in His amazing Grace
And led by the faithful Guide to see His gentle Face!

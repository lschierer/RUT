A Sweet Scent Fills My Senses

As I sit alone in complete darkness with tears spilling
The lovely, sweet scent of peace roses fills my senses
And I envision pale yellow rosebuds and blushing blooms
Gently cascading down over tall, privacy fences
Bringing pure joy to painful, broken, wide open hearts
Thoughtfully locked away and completely forgotten
Heralding a new dawn shining in the Light full of hope
To the despairing forsaken and desperate, down trodden.

On the Loose

 

On the Loose

My eyes are teary.
My mind is weary.
My heart is aching.
My nerves are quaking.
Temptation is on the loose!
My neck feels in a noose!
Life on earth
Is without mirth.
I need God's Mother
To show me how to suffer.
Time goes on .
I must be strong.
I need the Dove
To come help from above.

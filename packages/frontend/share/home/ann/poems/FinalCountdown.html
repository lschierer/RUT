Final Countdown

I am a rocket on the launch pad
The engines are ignited
And already revving up
The final countdown has begun
An excited anticipation awaits
My energy level becomes explosive
I can not contain it any longer
I am bursting at all my seams
I am quivering with quintessence
of Schierer happiness and jubilance
At the thought of the two of us
Walking together in love with Jesus!

The Nurse Shark

The Nurse Shark






Gracefully swimming with a swishing, slender tail
A brown nurse shark skims across a bed of sea grass
As smoothly as a clipper ship's full blown sail
Hungrily nosing into limestone crevices and cracks
Searching for a flailing fin or a torn and tattered tail
To make into a seafood lover's sumptious snack

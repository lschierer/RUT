My Main Mission

Raising my children is my main mission.
It requires lots oftime and much submission
To God for Him to work through me
To help my children be the dbest they can be!

It also requires patience and laughter
And knowledge that they'll still love me after
I've corrected and hounded and prodded
Them along to live a life like theSon of God led.

I must also remember that my actions will be "heard"
For my actions always speak louder than my words!
I'll pray to the Holy Spirit for wisdom and guidance
To follow God's Son with complete compliance!

I'll pray for my children each and every day
That God will bless them in whatever comes their way.
And if they have to face the evil one's way
I hope they'll remember to stop and pray!!!

All the Little I Have To Offer

Stabbing knives go through my chest so deep
And odd parts of my body jerk, jump and leap
Piercing through my wanted dreams and sleep
This pain seems for now to be mine to keep
Please dearest Mother of Tears and Sorrows
Help me to accept this pain now and tomorrow!
Please take all the little that i have to offer
And make it acceptable to our merciful Father!

A Desperate Desire to Defy Gravity

Today my dearest husband i am missing you so extremely terribly.
My soul, so alone, profusely pines away ardently and endlessly.
I crave deeply within my every crevice to feel the intensity
Of your firm fingertips stroking my skin so delicately.
I long to look at you and study every part of you intricately.
My heart yearns to hear your whispered words that imaginatively
Took us traveling throughout space and time to fantasy
Filled wonderlands where we wandered hand in hand in ecstasy.
I desperately desire to escape this earth's present, raw reality
To be a hummingbird flying so fast that i can defy gravity
For my soul to gently hover over yours as if you were a brightly
Colored flower so i could sip your nectar and rest intoxicatingly.

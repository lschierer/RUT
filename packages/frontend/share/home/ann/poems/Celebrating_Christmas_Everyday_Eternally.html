Celebrating Christmas Everyday Eternally




Sparkling, dazzling stars dangled
In the vastness of a coal black sky
Like millions of wireless, twinkle lights
Suspended by the All Merciful on High
To share with us His magnificent
Celebration of Christmas in eternity
To reveal to us His boundless loving
Hope for each soul in Humanity

Evergreen Faithful and Cerulean True

 

Evergreen Faithful and Cerulean True

With each colorfully, eye-splashing
Golden glow sunrise and rose petal sunset
I give my heartfelt thanks to our gracious God
For the shining Starlit day that we first met

You looked in
Past my lonely tears
And walked in
Past my worrisome fears
You ventured down in
So very, very, extremely deep
And bearing and sharing comfort
You began to carefully creep
Unexpectedly, you sat in my heart cozily
In an unfamiliar, unoccupied corner of space
You returned candle lit warmth to my contused soul
And brought back a smile on my saddened face
I felt such lovely, exquisite comfort
In your tenderly tight, encompassing embrace

Now, Without you, I wander around
Wishing for and ardently wanting a time and place
To once again see you
Your eyes gleaming with the glorious Rays of the Son’s Light
To once again speak with you
To hear your quiet words quell my worst fright
To finally, once again feel your presence
Passionately imbue
Filling me purely
With Love, so evergreen faithful and cerulean true!

In The Midnight

Happiness hides from me.
Fear fills my hurting heart.
Joy drowns in the depths of my painful sea
Of tears that pour down incessantly.
I am lost in anxiety
Still surrounded by suffering and sorrow.
Life contains no gaiety.
Loneliness leads me along the edge.
Evil looms overhead
Lurching
In the midnight which i dread.

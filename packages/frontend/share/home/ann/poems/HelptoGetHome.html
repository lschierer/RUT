Help to Get Home

Dearest Mother of the long Expected and Promised One
Please carefully place me under your compassionate cloak of protection
So that i may share The Love that is the cause of your purest joy
And feel the healing warmth of your hugs with your Heavenly, Holy Boy.
Please silence grief and sorrow’s cries and fear’s screams
So that my ears hear only the songs of the angels while your Son’s Beams
Flood my hurting heart with His intense loving forgiveness
And drench my sinful soul with His gentle mercy full of blessed kindness.
Please help me to trust in Divine Providence and Wisdom
Without needing to understand to accept The Way to home in His Kingdom.

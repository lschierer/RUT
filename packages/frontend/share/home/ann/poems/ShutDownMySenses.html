Shut Down My Senses

 

Shut Down My Senses

Dear sweet Jesus, please take control of my craving senses.
Detatch my mind and destroy all my fearfully built fences.
I beg you, dear Jesus, to please keep my eyes shut closed
That i may keep my focus solely on You, The One Who Rose.
Please take my voice and allow no audible speech or utterance
So my heart and soul can fill with Your message of deliverance.
Please deafen my ears to all the world's noise and sounds
That i may only hear You speak in the silence all around.
Please take away my desires to be touched and held
So that Your Blood may bathe my sinful soul and meld
My small sorrows and pain to Yours as a meager offering
For all those lonely, poor souls who are still suffering!

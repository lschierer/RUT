I Can Dream

If i can't hold you in my arms today so tenderly and tight
I can at least dream about you longingly and lovingly tonight.
When we are together, i feel so fully alive in every way.
Just being in your presence makes my pain pass away.
These feelings for you in my heart beat loud and true.
Each and every part of me yearns to show my love for you.
Love races through my veins burning intensely with desire.
My soul excitedly erupts, emitting love's eternal fire!
While i dream about you longingly and lovingly tonight
I can at least hope that you will soon be in my eyesight!

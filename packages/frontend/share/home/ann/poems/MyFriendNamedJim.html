My Friend Named Jim

 

My Friend Named Jim

I have a fantastic friend named Jim.
He is small in stature and very slim.
He currently lives in a federal prison
And he walks with the faith of Jesus Risen!
He smiles about his situation and suffering
As he knows the salvation that God is offering!
Jim is an extraordinary and exceptional friend
To me as he supports me when he sends
Written letters loaded with laughter
That raise my spirits right up to the rafters!
I pray that his beautiful wife, Babs and he
Will live in God's love and peace for eternity!

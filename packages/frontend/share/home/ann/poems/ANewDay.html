A New Day

 

A New Day

God's life can be felt in the dawning
Of this warm, spring-like morning.
His lavish love can be seen in the full moon's glow
And in the radiant sun's rays as they start to flow.
His voice can be heard in the solitude and stillness
As the day drifts in with His lasting,loving kindness.

Once Upon a December's Eve

 

Once Upon a December's Eve

Almost a year ago, once upon a cold, dark December's eve
A merry miracle of the Spirit's love was conceived and received
When two lonely souls, snowflake delicate and icicle fragile
Tenderly touched each other through their sad eyes and smiles!
Kind, warm words to comfort with care were softly spoken
While putting together pieces of their two hurting hearts broken
Both were amazed, shocked, star- gazed, and surprised
Neither could have imagined, dreamed, or ever realized
That a pyrotechnic display of friendship would joyfully dance
Across their two lives that would be affectionately enhanced!
The circling of their arms around each other assures and secures
Their close connection that one soul prays will eternally endure!

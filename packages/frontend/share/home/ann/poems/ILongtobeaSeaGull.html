I Long to be a Sea Gull

 

I Long to be a Sea Gull

I long to be a sea gull skimming the silvery surface of the sea
Searching for and reaping God’s bountiful feast that He’s provided for free
Gracefully waving my gray and white wings as i fly
Thanking my good God for all my generous gifts as i soar up into the sky!

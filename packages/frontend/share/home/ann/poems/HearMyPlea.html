Hear My Plea

 

Hear My Plea

I walk trembling
Stumbling
Almost crumbling
Along a perilous precipice!
I am no longer a novice
at this!
Yet, it seems more jagged
And i feel more ragged!!!
I am too tired to shout
Or cry out
Ever so silently
Utterly quietly
Within the deepest
Remotest
Recesses of my sorrowful soul
i ask Thee
to hear my plea
Please take me
Hold me
Mold me
Make me your own!!
I disown
All that i am !

Losing my Mind

My soul cries out to hear your kind and quiet voice.
Fearful tears from nightmares flow and my moist
Cheeks crave to feel your fingers firmly yet gently
Wipe them dry. I wishfully want and desire to see
You and spend some precious time in your physical presence.
In loneliness, i'm lost and losing my mind in your absence!
In the distance, it is your deep dark, sparkling eyes
That i see so vividly whenever i dream or fantasize!
I longingly would love to stretch and reach out
To touch your faith-filled face to erase my doubt.
I hurt with hunger to hold your hands, holy
And honest and to know that we can trust truly
In our God of Love to chase away loneliness, leaving no trace.
My heavy heart aches to feel your tender and warm embrace!

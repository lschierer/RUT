Not Alone

 

Not Alone

I stand completely alone
Underneath His Throne
He doesn’t call me a subject
I am not considered a reject
He is not here to deject me
He is not here to inject me
I am not a suspect
Of dirt, i am but a speck
He is The One, all Perfect
He deserves all my respect
He deserves my everything
Yet, He expects nothing
He is so purely loving
No demands, no shoving
He is so extremely merciful
His gifts to us are so plentiful
His kindness overflows
His goodness constantly shows
He is so intimate
Yet, He is infinite!
He specializes in the impossible
Yet, His command is so simple
Love Him and love others
Treat everyone as our brothers
I am never really all alone
His Life in me has been sown!

Everything I Ever Dreamed

 

Everything I Ever Dreamed

I knew you were sent by our God above
As you were everything and more than i had ever dreamed of.
When your twinkling, green eyes gazed into mine
I could feel our two souls sparkle, sensually entwine
And leap from our bodies to spiritually embrace.
Our hearts rapidly beat as if we had just run a race!
I knew from our relationship's very beginning that our
Love would transcend our time on Earth's last hour.
God had given us a share of His love for eternity.
He meant for our love to go beyond with the Trinity.

I dream about and pray for my favorite fantasy
To become my most cherished and relished reality.
I long to see your shining face with no trace
Of the pain that is now is replaced by God's grace.
I wish for a chance to talk and for you to touch
My cheeks tenderly which you loved to do so much.
I long for our souls to lay with each other side by side
Until day can't break, evening can't fall, and night can't slide
Into a new day dawning with a place to run and hide.
I long to run across the brilliant bridge that is endless and wide.

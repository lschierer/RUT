Like a Shooting Star

 

Like a Shooting Star

Our love was radiantly romantic, beaming bright
Full of sparkling happiness during the night.
Our love twinkled like a star for miles afar.
Our love left like a shimmering shooting star
In a matter of swiftly sweeping seconds
Our love's light streaked across the heavens
Shining
Spiraling
Free- falling
Down
Now, our light is out and no where to be found!

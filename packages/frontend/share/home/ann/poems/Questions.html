Questions

Why did you have to leave so early
Why couldn't you stay one more day
Why did all of our dreams die so quickly
Why couldn't medicine have helped in some way?

How do i let go of you
How do i live with a broken heart
How do i know what is best to do
How do i accept this new part?

What will my future hold
What is my final destiny
What will i be as i grow old
What is God's Will for me??

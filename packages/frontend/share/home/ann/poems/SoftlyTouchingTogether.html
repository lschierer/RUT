Softly Touching Together

 

Softly Touching Together

Shadows woven from memories surround me
The coldness of loneliness still astounds me
I so long to feel the warmth of your cheek pressed next to mine
For with your loving touch, i knew everything would be fine!
You were able to slowly hold back the blackest, darkest catastrophe
And your words soothed my soul like a violin solo in a symphony!
No length of time can ever erase this homesick heartache!
I just fervently pray that the New Dawn of Creation will soon awake
For i desirously long to see my beloved in the Light of the new day
And our matching hearts and mated souls to joyfully unite and stay
Softly touching together
For always and forever!

My Faithful Friend

I miss my friend, Kathy so very much.
I miss her phone calls to keep in touch.
I love her for her quick and sharp sense of humor
And i know she will never ever spread a rumor.
She's always upbeat and exponentially full of energy.
We work well together, like two muscles in synergy.
My friend is faithful to her Catholic faith, family,
And friends and always offers her gracious hospitality.
She is strong in spirit and so very sentimental.
Her smile is sincere and she'll defend you in a battle.
She is small in stature but her heart is huge.
She is the cornerstone of her loving family's refuge.

Wrong Person Left

 

Wrong Person Left

I am the only female here.
My presence is not needed or wanted, i fear.
I seem to interfere with all their plans.
They resent that my life and not Dan's
Life was spared and left with them here.
I 'd do anything to trade right now with you,Dear.
And then they have life more as it was.
Life wouldn't seem crazy and all a buzz!!

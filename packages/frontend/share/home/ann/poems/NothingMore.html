Nothing More

 

Nothing More

A green-eyed guy generously gave a gleeful, brown-eyed girl
All of his true love that caused her heart to completely unfurl!
She passionately wanted for positively nothing more
Than for him to unlock her never opened door
And to make a safe and secure home in her soul
Sharing God’s Love together long and warmly as slow burning coal
Sharing God’s limitless gift of Life
As forever friends united for always, eternally, as man and wife!

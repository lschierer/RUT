Special Day

The dawning of this special day has yet to arrive
Yet my heart and soul overflow with excitement
And anticipation of seeing my friend live!
I've given sorrow, doubt, and despair extended leave
As i'll have the Face of God and His Mother
With me at my side to help me sincerely believe
That i can confidently meet every challenge ahead of me
If i just decide to take up His cross and follow her lead
In my everyday, ordinary life of what i think, say and see!

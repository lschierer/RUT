I Know God

 

I Know God

I have seen the face of God
In your kind face.
I have held the hand of God
With your strong hand.
I have heard the voice of God
Through your caring voice.
I have touched the Heart of God
When i hugged you closely.
I know God is really and truly present
By your physical presence.

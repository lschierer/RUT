All I Want to Do

All I Want to Do





All i want to do is to be able to show you and simply give you all of my love
All i want to do is to hold you, standing in the sparkling of the stars above

When loneliness stretches each second into seemingly hours that we're apart
When grief grabs a death- grip hold of and strangles my doleful, doubting heart

All i want to do is to be able to show you and simply give you all of my love
All i want to do is to hold you, standing in the sparkling of the stars above

When sorrow stabs you successively, going deeper and deeper into your gut
When drowning despair makes all your doors appear to be tightly sealed shut

All i want to do is to be able to show you and simply give you all of my love
All i want to do is to hold you, standing in the sparkling of the stars above

When monsterous troubles terrorize your mind like a speeding train tornado
When your soul has been smashed and pulled apart as if it was just play dough

All i want to do is to be able to show you and simply give you all of my love
All i want to do is to hold you, standing in the sparkling of the stars above

Crazy Quilt-stitched Patchwork



A vegetable garden patchwork of vividly remembered, cherished times
Crazy quilt-stitched in imaginatively choreographed silk thread colors
Wraps around my suddenly, swiftly sinking into the deep blue sea soul
Surrrounding me like a cuddly, long-haired cat full of consoling purrs

Romantic as vintage postcards of  palm treed sunsets in Old Key West
Silly as the Sunday funny paper's Katz and Jammer Kids cartoons
My memories light up my mind like Broadway's dazzling, neon signs
Holding colorful love much more rare than a month full of blue moons

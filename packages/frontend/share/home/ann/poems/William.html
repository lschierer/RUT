William

William is a whimsical whirlwind
Of enthusiasm, energy, and emotion
His coming home causes commotion
And quiet is now but a remembrance
As William's vibrant and vivacious presence
Sets off fireworks with just a glance.
With Will around, i can't help but dance
As his love for life is infectious
And his laughter is extremely contagious.
I am so very blessed to have a son like him.
May God help him to grow in grace and wisdom!

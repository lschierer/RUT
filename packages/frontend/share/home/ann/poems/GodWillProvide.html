God Will Provide

 

God Will Provide

I feel so strange
My life's full of change
I've been involved in a battle
The fight caused me to be rattled
I feel fairly confident
That God is my occupant
I trust that He won't let me down
He'll keep my feet on good ground
The Son will continue to light the way
He'll help me to never wander astray
The Dove will fly with calm, warm winds
And He'll forgive me of all my sins
As long as I have faith and true contrition
And promise to bring His will to fruition.

Such a Divine Loving Creator

 

Such a Divine Loving Creator

Revealing, foreboding and breath- taking
My soul stirs at this awesome awakening
Of the time of shadows and sparkling stars
Of the night before the wind will begin to blow cars
The striking sky is a queer, hurricane blue
With sizzling hot pink clouds making streaks all through
I stand silently and behold God’s infinite magnificence
And realize immediately my life’s entire insignificance
Without the Grace of such a Divine loving Creator.
Thank you God for also being such a patient Educator!

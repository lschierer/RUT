Astounded and Awed

 

Astounded and Awed

God is the center of this immense universe.
His perfect wisdom is the connective force
That holds myriads of celestial bodies together
Within a distance of unfathomable measure.
I am astounded and awed by this cosmic system
In a galaxy that appears no bigger than a pin
Point of a precious,simple, bright light
>From a far off distance in the middle of the night.

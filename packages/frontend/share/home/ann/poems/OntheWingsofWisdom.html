On the Wings of Wisdom

 

On the Wings of Wisdom

I awake from a cold and desolate dream.
My reality is as it did seem
Worse!
Lonliness envelopes my body
Suffocating my soul
Strangling my heart
Depressing all bloodflow
My chaotic mind
Like a huge traffic snarl during rush hour
Longs to be free
To flee!!
To fly away
On the wings of Wisdom
To where my heart won't hurt!

Christmas Cheer

 

Christmas Cheer

Lacy, white angel wings, sticky, minty candy canes
Misty eyed mistletoe kisses
Silver and red tied up ribbons, green and gold bows
Two sweet little misses
Sons with twinkling eyes and sparkling smiles so bright
Old people giggling gaily
I long, i crave, i wish i could wrap up our lonely hearts
With Christmas cheer daily!

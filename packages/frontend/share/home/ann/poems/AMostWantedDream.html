A Most Wanted Dream

When the golden glittery moonlight gleams
You wander into my most wanted dream
Setting the lonely night colorfully aglow
Like a shimmery and shining rainbow
Full of hopeful possibilities, unlimited
By the generous mercy of God, unmerited!

Dark clouds of destructive storms fall behind
Stars, like diamonds that sparkle and shine.
You ever so tenderly unravel and gently tug
To untie my heartstrings with your huge hug.
Like rain, love pours in and floods my soul
I again feel complete and wonderfully whole!

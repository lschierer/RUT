The Intimate King

 

The Intimate King

Dear Sweet Jesus lovingly and longingly wants me.
He graciously invites me and warmly welcomes me.
He comes down off His Heavenly throne on the altar.
Through hands, He comes in bread and wine with water.
He is truly the ultimate Christ and the most intimate King.
In the Blessed Holiest Eucharist, to sinful me He brings
Total healing, merciful forgiveness and unmerited love.
I receive abundant grace and abiding peace from the Dove
Who fills my sorrowful heart with a powerful burning
For The Way and The Truth and a passionate yearning
To share this awesome and life-lifting love affair with all
People of all races in all places in my so small, small
Way of trying to unconditionally love with no judging
Just hoping and praying while giving and not begrudging!

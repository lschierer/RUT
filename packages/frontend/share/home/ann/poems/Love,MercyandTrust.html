Love, Mercy and Trust

In my dark soul's cold confinement
Dripping with despair
Through the most Blessed Sacrament
A most perfect love affair
God holds onto my heart and hand.
With His tender touch
I clutch onto His as hard as i can!
I need Him so much!
He offers His blanket of security
A Blessed Lady's cape
Woven with His wonderful mercy.
I cannot escape
All He asks is that i totally trust
Giving up my will
That eternal life can arise from dust
Life is still a thrill!

I Long for The One I Love the Most!

Dear Blessed Mother, Holy Mary
Tonight i feel so very weak and weary.
I kneel prostrate before you at your feet
Please ask your Son to make me complete.
I long for His loving intimacy that i only find
In the Holy Sacrament most Divine!
I hunger to feel Him inside of my body
And soul just as you, my dear Mary
Felt Him kick inside your precious womb
Where He was snug and safe from all doom.
And just as you dreamed to hold Him
And to see His Face in the stable light so dim
I, too, dream to hold Him and to be held
By Him as my heart and soul are meld
To His when i receive the precious Host!
Please hug Him and tell Him i love Him the most!

Longing To Be a Reindeer

I long to serve as one of the Holy Spirit's reindeer
Dancing and prancing merrily without tears or fear
Pulling a sleigh full of His hope, love, and good cheer
Making high-flying leaps and bounds through the clear
Starry night to swiftly deliver the gifts to souls so near
To departing to go to live with The One they hold dear!

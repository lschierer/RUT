On This Second Day of June, 2004

On this second day of June, my dearest second born son, William
As you spread your wings to fly over our sweet Lord’s enthralling,
earthly kingdom
I pleadingly pray that the dear Holy Comforter will be with you and in
you these next six weeks
To keep you safe in mind, body and soul as you cruise across new valleys
and overtop new peaks
I pray that you’ll constantly remember that you are accountable for all
your words and deeds
To Divine, Triune Love, your precious Maker and merciful Provider for
all your needs
I pray that you will enjoy exciting, eye- widening experiences, engage
with enchanting people
Explore the extraordinary environment and exercise your expertise with
both young and feeble
Please remember that no matter what, right now, forever and ever, and
for always
I will love you with motherly tenderness that travels straight away,
sideways, and all byways
Not ever one bit daunted by disaster or disloyalty or dejection or danger
Strengthened in the Deliverer of the true message who was born in a manger
A motherly love that transcends all time and any type of distance
And just grows more deeply and dear with every moment and in each instance!

Only Possible

Just as God Who is our Father parted the wide Sea called Red
So He did give us His only Son to resurrect from the dead
Just as our Three in One God dried up the River Jordan
So all my terrible sins and many faults He does pardon
With incredible strength and mercy streaming down from above
All made so visible, all made only possible in His infinite Love

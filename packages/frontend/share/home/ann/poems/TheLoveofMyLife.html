The Love of My Life

You were the love of my life.
I thoroughly enjoyed being your wife.
I loved the way you kissed me "Good Morning"
And how you held my hand while snoring.
I loved going out to breakfast on Sundays
And i disliked you leaving for work on Mondays.
I loved taking walks with you along the paths
And i admired your ability to do higher Math.

You were the love of my life.
I thoroughly enjoyed being your wife.
I loved the way you cracked my back
And I appreciated your skill of how to pack.
I loved walking around our yard,looking at flowers
And how you would want to love me in the wee hours.
I loved planting our garden in the Spring
And bringing in the vegetables after harvesting.

You were the love of my life.
I thoroughly enjoyed being your wife.
I loved sitting next to you in a restaurant
And how you would ask me to select the font.
I loved helping you out when you volunteered
And i was amazed at how time with you disappeared.
I loved lying in bed and discussing our future
And how you stood by when our sons needed sutures.

You were the love of my life.
I thoroughly enjoyed being your wife.
I loved watching you coach our guys in baseball
And i admired you as an umpire making a close call.
I loved sitting on the front porch looking at fireflies
And how you teased me about the way i made my pies.
I loved watching you set off fireworks after dark
And eating picnic lunches with you at a park.

You were the love of my life.
I thoroughly enjoyed being your wife.
I loved going with you to our swimming pool
And I admired your ability to use every tool.
I loved going shopping with you for a dress
And i admired your patience whenever i would stress.
I loved the way that you would look at me
And how you would hold my hand as we snorkeled undersea.

You were the love of my life.
I thoroughly enjoyed being your wife.
I loved you as a boyfriend and then fiancé
I loved you as a bridegroom on our wedding day.
I loved you as a proud father of each of our sons.
I loved you as a confidant in all situations.
I will love you forever and always through eternity.
I hope to spend time with you and the most Holy Trinity!

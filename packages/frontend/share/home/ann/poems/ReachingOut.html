Reaching Out

I reach out for your hand now
As i need your help more now
Than at any other time in my entire life!
I am struggling with sin and temptation
And surrendering my sorrow and desperation!
I am afraid
I have not paid
Enough attention to your signs.
I keep falling down in between the lines.
Dear sweet Jesus, please help me to surrender
And to never forget but always remember
That You love me unconditionally, for always
And You want me with You at the end of my days.
Dear Mother Mary, i humbly beseech you to hear
My heart and ask your Son to heal and to sear
His permanent mark on my soul as a sign of slavery
For i crave to be consecrated to you at Calvary.

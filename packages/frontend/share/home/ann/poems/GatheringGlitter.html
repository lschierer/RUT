Gathering Glitter

 

Gathering Glitter
I am as full of joy as a Cardinal
Who has found a feeder of fennel.
I am as giddy
As a calico kitty.
I am as fancy free as a fairy
Who flits about while making merry.
If i had wings and could fly
I'd fly way up into the sky
To gather God's glitter amongst
The twinkling stars and sparkling hunks
of ice balls that He tosses and throws
To keep me attentive and on my toes!

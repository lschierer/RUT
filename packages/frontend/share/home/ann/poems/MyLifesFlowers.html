My Life's Flowers

 

My Life's Flowers

Friends and family are my life's flowers
That bloom profusely during and after showers.
The Master Gardener planted them as seeds at first.
He divinely provided for all their needs and thirst.
His love nourished stems for strength and support.
Each one is different, some are tall and some short.

Friends and family are my life's flowers
That bloom profusely during and after showers.
When wind, hail, or torrents of rain hit their stem,
The Master Gardener blesses them so that they may bend.
Their vivid and varied colors radiate the love from the Son.
I revel in their fragrances when i am around them and among.

Friends and family are my life's flowers
That bloom profusely during and after showers.
I admire and feel blessed to see their individual beauty
And i'm privileged to be with them though very unworthy.
These precious flowers are rooted deeply within my heart.
They even survive when i run over them with my garden cart!

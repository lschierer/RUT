Total Union

What is happening to my heart, i cannot ignore.
I have not ever felt love like this before.
When i receive Jesus in Holy Communion
I feel as if our bodies are in total union.
Jesus is in me, throughout my whole being.
He is all around me in the air i am breathing.
I can feel Him holding my heart in His hand.
He surrounds my body like a warm, acrylic afghan!
His love enters into my heart like lightning bolts
Travel down a tree trunk releasing their volts.
Jesus' love burns deep inside my heart with desire
To share and spread His Holy Spirit's fire!

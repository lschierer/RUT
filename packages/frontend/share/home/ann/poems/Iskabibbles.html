Iskabibbles

 

Iskabibbles

Where are we going for breakfast today?
Iskabibbles
Why are we going in the car this way?
Iskabibbles
What are you making with that wood and glue?
Iskabibbles
What are you working on downstairs until two?
Iskabibbles
Whenever Dan said Iskabibbles
There could be no more questions or quibbles.
How could you ask or argue about something
About which you knew he was not answering!
